Nueva Calendar Cleaner: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Nueva Calendar Cleaner is a Chrome extension that hides Google Calendar event chips whose titles match rules you choose. This policy explains what it does with your data: in short, nothing leaves your browser.

What the extension reads

The extension runs only on https://calendar.google.com/*. It reads the titles and visible text of event chips on the page so it can hide the ones that match your rules. This happens entirely inside your browser. The extension does not delete, edit, or unsubscribe from any calendar event.

What the extension stores

Your matching rules are saved with chrome.storage.local in your Chrome profile on your device. They are not synced through Chrome sync and are never sent anywhere. They stay until you change them or uninstall the extension.

What the extension collects or shares

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, external network requests, remote code, or server operated by the developer.

Other services

This policy covers the extension only. Google Calendar and Chrome have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If the extension ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
