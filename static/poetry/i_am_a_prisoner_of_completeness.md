i am a prisoner of completeness;
half breaths and forgotten actions drag me down.
not perfection. i just cringe where there is
a missing piece—
a tiny piece—
even miniscule.

the line of five and four needs one more,
or to be rearranged in three threes; 
prime numbers are satanic.
essays become caveat lists
—to be clear, i don't mean this to be an insult
—please don't pin a single misrepresented argument on the entire existence of my being i didn't mean it!!
i'm a fan of "although...", "notably...", "keep in mind...", "it is the case that..."
tmi is chronic

my remembrall blaring red brings a tightness in the chest that derails focus
forgetting ideas is losing pieces of myself
even tiny pieces
pieces i'll never be able to share with another
piles of piles of notes i dont read but feel extremely good.
<u>extremely</u> good.
my website is extremely unfocused.
<u>extremely</u> unfocused.

at times i glimpse beyond the bars of my prison
when i shrug at my remembrall or accept a half breath
but i quickly look away.
besides, its pretty cozy in here :)
