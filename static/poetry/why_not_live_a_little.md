what is life without its miseries?
heaven: all the delicacies of the world&mdash;
at the tips of your fingers;
gilded.
like a sweet apple.
purgatory: where are the valleys to shape the mountains?
instead there is overwhelming
flatness
hell: sometimes too easy becomes meaningless;
sometimes one yearns for
a struggle&mdash;
a thorn to evade with an adrenaline rush,
a game of beer pong.
sometimes one feels.
i say to pain and embarrassment and sadness and aimlessness, *why not live a little? if not to feel, what makes me human?*
