alas, if it could react even just a bit
to my theorems or dilemmas&mdash;to
give me a sense of comfort
the cold and rigid surface like
shiny table tops in a diner
do no such thing
somehow, i draw warmth from it's cold surfaces
heaps and heaps of it
i reside in the whiteboard, no matter how
grainy it is with unwiped marker ink
