Innovative Learning Conference, The Nueva School

A conference on teaching and learning that Nueva has hosted about every two years since 2007. Around 600 educators attended in 2026. Past speakers include Sal Khan and Fei-Fei Li.

How Nueva Fosters Innovators and Builders (Students' Perspectives)

March 13, 2026. A 75-minute workshop for teachers, with Dirac Ge.

Dirac and I proposed this session. We went through the specific things Nueva does to grow students who build things, from classes and clubs to how teachers run office hours, drawing on our own experience and on interviews with teachers. The teachers in the room then talked about what they could bring back to their own schools.

The Mystery of Consciousness

March 15, 2026. Crothers Memorial Hall.

Why subjective experience is still unexplained. I covered the hard problem and philosophical zombies, compared three current theories (integrated information theory, global workspace theory, and quantum microtubules), showed where each one falls short, and ended with what I think.
